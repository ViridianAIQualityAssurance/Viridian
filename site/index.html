01

Product

A higher score is an observation. It is not automatically a defensible conclusion.

Viridian separates what happened from what the evidence is allowed to prove.

Evidence identity

Bind results to the model, dataset, evaluator, code, environment and run identities that actually produced them.

Comparator integrity

Detect when baseline and candidate are no longer materially comparable, even when both still produce plausible scores.

Evaluator integrity

Treat the judge or evaluator as part of the measurement system, with its own identity, failures and qualification boundaries.

Missingness & UNKNOWN

Keep dropped rows, failed judges and absent identities visible instead of silently converting missing evidence into confidence.

Confirmation boundaries

Distinguish development evidence from protected confirmation so iterative tuning does not inherit final-decision authority.

Claim scope

Return the strongest statement the current evidence can support—and exactly what additional evidence would change it.

04

Security

Security claims are held to the same evidence standard.

Qualified where measured. Explicit where unknown.

Current qualified boundary: trusted-local.

Advanced V5 is experimental-assurance infrastructure, not a hardened malware sandbox. Hostile native-code containment remains outside the qualified security scope.

The architecture uses persistent local SQLite stores for structured evidence, provenance, recovery and reconstruction. Deployment-specific network, privacy and credential properties must be measured for the actual package and integration.

NOT CLAIMED

SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO/IEC 42001 or NIST certification.

NOT CLAIMED

Universal no-phone-home behavior before packaged network testing.

NOT CLAIMED

Qualification transfer from the pinned R3 envelope to arbitrary customer stacks.
